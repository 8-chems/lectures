Chapter 1 — Introduction to Data Science

Definitions, workflows, ecosystem and methodology

data-science
foundations
Understand what data science is, learn key workflows (CRISP-DM, KDD, TDSP), and explore the tools and roles in a data science project.

Chapter Summary

Data Science combines a collection of methods, tools, and techniques aimed at extracting useful knowledge or reusable patterns from raw data. This process involves several key stages — acquiring, preparing, modeling, and visualizing data, followed by deploying appropriate analytical tools.

In a world where most decisions are made based on partial information and uncertain outcomes, data science helps reduce uncertainty by applying robust computational methods.

TipSkills you will acquire in this chapter
  • Definition and scope of data science
  • Key workflows: CRISP-DM, KDD, TDSP, Agile Data Science
  • Roles and tools in a data science ecosystem
  • Overview of the 6 main stages of a DS project

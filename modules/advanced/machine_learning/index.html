Machine Learning — Professional Program

advanced
A 23-week program from ML foundations to deployment-ready skills and a capstone project.

🎯 Program Overview

Duration: 23 Weeks (~5.5 Months) Format: 2 × 1.5h lectures/week (Theory + Walkthrough) + 4–6 hrs/week practice Outcome: Capstone-ready ML practitioner with theory-based modeling skills, practical pipelines, and a professional portfolio.

📅 Curriculum

Week 1 — ML Foundations & Workflow

What is ML? Supervised vs Unsupervised vs Reinforcement Learning

Week 2 — Data Preprocessing & Pipelines

Handling missing data, detecting outliers

Week 3 — Math & Information Theory

Linear algebra review: vectors, dot products, matrix multiplication

Week 4 — Linear Models for Regression

Linear Regression: formula, interpretation, closed-form solution

Week 5 — Logistic Regression & Classification

Logistic function, decision boundaries, sigmoid

Week 6 — Model Evaluation

Regression metrics: MAE, MSE, RMSE, R²

Week 7 — Bias–Variance Tradeoff

Underfitting vs overfitting; bias-variance decomposition

Week 8 — Data Sampling & Validation Techniques

Hold-out split, stratified sampling

Week 9 — k-Nearest Neighbors

KNN for regression and classification

Week 10 — Decision Trees

Splitting criteria: Gini, Entropy

Week 11 — Random Forests & Bagging

Bagging: theory and bias reduction

Week 12 — Boosting Algorithms

Boosting concepts: ensemble of weak learners

Week 13 — Modern Boosting Libraries

XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost

Week 14 — Feature Engineering & Feature Selection

Custom transformers, interaction features

Week 15 — Imbalanced & Noisy Data

Resampling: SMOTE, undersampling

Week 16 — Clustering & Dimensionality Reduction

Clustering: K-Means, DBSCAN, Hierarchical

Week 17 — Neural Networks & MLPs

Perceptron, hidden layers, activation functions (ReLU, sigmoid, tanh)

Week 18 — CNNs & Image Classification

Convolution, pooling, deep architectures (LeNet, AlexNet, VGG)

Week 19 — NLP & Time Series

NLP: tokenization, stemming, TF-IDF, Word2Vec, GloVe

Week 20 — Full ML Pipeline: Mini Project

Dataset selection and objective definition

Week 21 — Capstone Development (Part 1)

Final project scoping and planning

Week 22 — Capstone Development (Part 2)

Full modeling and evaluation pipeline

Week 23 — Capstone Presentations & Career Prep

Project showcase and peer feedback
